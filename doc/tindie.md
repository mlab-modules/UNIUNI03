#### What is it?
The UNIUNI03 is a versatile, compact universal PCB with a pad spacing of 1.27mm. Designed for flexibility, this PCB can be cut to the desired length, making it ideal for various SMD component applications.

#### Why did you make it?
We created the UNIUNI03 to address the need for a flexible, customizable PCB solution suitable for SMD components. This module provides a practical and adaptable platform for electronic enthusiasts and professionals to solder a prototype of designs efficiently.

#### What makes it special?
The UNIUNI03 stands out due to its universal compatibility and customizable length. Its 1.27mm pad spacing is optimized for SMD components, ensuring a reliable and convenient solution for a wide range of electronic projects. The ability to cut the PCB to the required length enhances its adaptability, making it a must-have for any electronics toolkit.

### Key Features
- **Universal PCB:** Suitable for a variety of SMD components with 1.27mm pad spacing.
- **Customizable Length:** Can be cut to the desired length to fit your project needs.
- **Compact Design:** Small size makes it perfect for space-constrained applications, like sensors.
